Who I am

I'm a passionate car enthusiast and the founder of Ura Studio, a detailing business dedicated to precision, quality, and craftsmanship. My work reflects my belief that attention to detail is what separates good from exceptional, whether it's restoring a car's finish or building digital experiences.

Currently, I'm focused on expanding my business while also developing my skills in Front-end web development with technologies such as React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. My long-term ambition is to combine my eye for detail, design, and functionality into both the automotive and digital worlds.
