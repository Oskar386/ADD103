Cars

I have been into cars since I was a kid. I used to play with toy cars for hours, imagining I was building and tuning them. Later, I started helping my dad and grandpa in the garage, learning tools, patience, and small repairs. Now I work on real cars, and that same excitement never disappeared. My long-term goal is to build a drift car and eventually step into professional drifting. Ken Block was a huge inspiration for me. His creativity, control, and style showed that driving is not just skill, but expression and vision.

For me, cars are not just machines. They reflect personality, style, and attention to detail. Everyone builds their car differently, and each one becomes its own piece of work. Some take a clean and OEM route, others go bold and creative. Both are right because every build tells a story about the person behind it and what they value.
